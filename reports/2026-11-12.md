Jensen Huang [resigned as Nvidia's CEO, effective immediately](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition). The company's own release credits him with 33 years of building it. Meanwhile a security firm found [over 1,100 public GitHub files holding real secrets](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=153s), and almost half came from commits co-authored by Claude.

Here is what happened in AI and DevOps this cycle.

Nvidia's board named CFO Colette Kress [interim CEO, and she keeps the CFO job too](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-ceo-transition). Huang also left the board. The board named Harvey C. Jones, a former Synopsys CEO, its first-ever Chair, and it will look at inside and outside candidates for a permanent CEO. The board says operations, customer commitments and the product roadmap continue without interruption. The first test comes fast: Nvidia reports third-quarter results on November 17 as planned.

Anthropic opened its IPO page and put the numbers up front: [17.0 billion dollars of revenue in the third quarter](https://ipo.anthropic.com/us/), more than 12x a year earlier. More than 11,900 customers now pay it at least 100,000 dollars a year. The same page shows a net loss of 23.3 billion dollars. Anthropic says 21.9 billion of that is a non-cash charge, because its own valuation went up. Shares are expected to trade on Nasdaq as "ANT" on November 19, with the price set the day before.

The Claude Code leak starts with a convenience. When you approve a command with "yes, don't ask again", [Claude Code saves that command to a file](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=116s). A database command can carry your password in plain text. The file usually lands in the global gitignore, but [not always in the project one](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=131s), so an agent commit can push it. Security company Aperio [searched public repos and found over 104,000 of these files](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=153s). Anthropic's head of Claude Code says the team is [looking into it and that auto mode might help](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=176s). The video comes from Firecrawl, which pitches its own search tool, and it says the password problem was [first reported in August 2025](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=208s). Deleting the file won't help, because it stays in your git history.

A second Claude Code bug leaks your email. Your signed-in address [sits in the session context for author tags](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=50s). When Claude fetches a page with curl, it [may put that real email in the user agent](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=64s). That is the string a client sends to name itself to a web server. One user says they now get dozens of scam emails a day, but they can't prove Claude Code caused it. Lydia from the Claude Code team [apologized and promised a fix in the next release](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uLJ4jfEnksk&t=94s).

Holiday Extras, a 44-year-old UK travel company, [gave every employee Codex](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vu3YlUMkvyg&t=43s), from the CEO to the call center. The last app rebuild took over a year. This one was [a two-day prototype and two weeks to production](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vu3YlUMkvyg&t=68s). OpenAI's case study says the new app [cut its error rate from 19% to 9% and lifted open-to-book conversion 32.5%](https://openai.com/business/customer-stories/holidays-extras-codex). Staff ship through an in-house [Builders Platform, a safe place for anyone to deploy](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vu3YlUMkvyg&t=49s), and one longtime accountant [rebuilt some of the finance systems himself](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vu3YlUMkvyg&t=81s).

Nader Dabit says the team at Basis is [running agent sessions of up to 20 days and going all in on cloud agents](https://x.com/dabit3/status/2120792028485501209). He calls it a picture of the accounting world now. His line: AI won't disrupt your business, but a team of AI power users may.

The CNCF explained why Kubernetes struggles with AI training. The default scheduler [places pods one at a time on any eligible node](https://www.cncf.io/blog/2026/11/12/gang-scheduling-and-topology-aware-placement-for-ai-training-in-kubernetes/). A training job needs every pod to start together and to sit close on the network. Gang scheduling, which starts all of a job's pods at once or none, stops GPUs from sitting allocated but idle. Kueue adds topology-aware placement over three domains: block, rack and host. The trap is that dashboards look fine, because infrastructure metrics measure activity, not training throughput.

An IBM Technology whiteboard talk asks one question about ops agents: [what is the system doing right now?](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hvnPWgfmvyI&t=10s) RAG, retrieval-augmented generation, gives an agent the runbooks, but [a runbook may be months old](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hvnPWgfmvyI&t=90s). In IBM's example, a banking balance check [runs from an API layer through a queue to a mainframe](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hvnPWgfmvyI&t=203s). Add traces, metrics and change records, and the agent can point to a slow middleware call. It can also flag a growing queue and a [config change just before the slowdown](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hvnPWgfmvyI&t=270s). IBM is careful here: [none of those facts alone prove root cause](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hvnPWgfmvyI&t=276s), so the human still decides.

Isomorphic Labs president Max Jaderberg wants to move drug discovery onto a computer. The No Priors host puts a drug at [about 15 years and 1.5 billion dollars](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IiKf3BgBezE&t=1039s), and most of that goes to clinical trials. Jaderberg says drugs mostly fail [in the clinic, from toxicity nobody saw coming](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IiKf3BgBezE&t=1103s). Rat studies, the regulator's gold standard, are [only about 30% predictive of human toxicity](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IiKf3BgBezE&t=1285s). He jokes that he'd send an AI model that weak back for more work. So Isomorphic generates its own lab data, and he says it sees [strong data scaling laws](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IiKf3BgBezE&t=1221s).

That's the cycle. Go check your project gitignore for that Claude Code file, give your ops agent live traces, and see you at noon.
